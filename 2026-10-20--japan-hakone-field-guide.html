Committed stops, not reserved time slots. Two scenic parks on the existing Golden Course loop: Kojiri first near the north end of Lake Ashi, then Onshi at the south end after the sightseeing cruise. Both are free-access parks; the exact viewpoint will be chosen on the day based on privacy, visibility and autumn colours. Don't plan a torii photo queue.

Gora Park Craft House — bracelets and keyrings (optional, unbooked)

Gora Park entry is free with Hakone Freepass, but workshops are paid separately. Two different activities fit the preferred keepsakes:

  • Vintage Beads Workshop — bracelet/accessory making: from ¥3,080 per person, ~30 min check-in + ~60 min making. Bracelets have an additional cost above the base price, as do some charms/beads; finished accessories typically go home the same day. The no-surcharge base offering is a brooch/dreamcatcher strap, not necessarily a bracelet or keyring.
  • Laser Engraving Workshop — personalized keyring: from ¥2,420 per person, keyring included at base price, roughly 30 min reception/selection + 30 min machining. Other items add fees; same-day keepsake.
  • Reservation rule: Official Craft House FAQ. Walk-ins possible if space exists, but reserved guests take priority. The 17 Nov 13:00 onsen is fixed, so only start a class if you can finish, collect hotel luggage, descend to Yumoto and arrive at Yuryo by ~12:45. Otherwise skip the workshop. Booking is optional, not required.
