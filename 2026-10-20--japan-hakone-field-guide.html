Gora Park Craft House — bracelets and keyrings (optional, unbooked)

Gora Park entry is free with Hakone Freepass, but workshops are paid separately. Two different activities fit the preferred keepsakes:

  • Vintage Beads Workshop — bracelet/accessory making: from ¥3,080 per person, ~30 min check-in + ~60 min making. Bracelets have an additional cost above the base price, as do some charms/beads; finished accessories typically go home the same day. The no-surcharge base offering is a brooch/dreamcatcher strap, not necessarily a bracelet or keyring.
  • Laser Engraving Workshop — personalized keyring: from ¥2,420 per person, keyring included at base price, roughly 30 min reception/selection + 30 min machining. Other items add fees; same-day keepsake.
  • Reservation rule: Official Craft House FAQ. Walk-ins possible if space exists, but reserved guests take priority. The 17 Nov 13:00 onsen is fixed, so only start a class if you can finish, collect hotel luggage, descend to Yumoto and arrive at Yuryo by ~12:45. Otherwise skip the workshop. Booking is optional, not required.

Ryokan meal/bath details — protect the included value

Tokinoyu Setsugetsuka (Gora), 15–17 Nov, 2 adults, 36 m² non-smoking room, two single beds; large bed request not guaranteed. Booking's per-night two-meal plan means dinner 15 Nov, breakfast + dinner 16 Nov, breakfast 17 Nov for both guests. Check-in usually 15:00, checkout 11:00.

  • Dinner: ordinarily 17:15 or 19:45 seating, assigned by hotel on arrival / availability. Do not assume late dinner, plan to return by ~17:00 on 16 Nov unless later sitting is confirmed. Kaiseki or Japanese beef shabu-shabu/sushi set, choice usually in order of check-in.
  • Breakfast: usually from 07:00, Japanese style, subject to date-specific adjustment.
  • Baths: in-room open-air bathtub uses ordinary heated water, while the property also has natural-spring communal baths and three free, no-reservation private open-air hot-spring baths, available when vacant. Enjoy these during the ryokan stay as included value; the separate 17 Nov Yuryo bath is already booked.
  • Allergies: hotel changed its food-allergy approach on 1 July 2025, offers a reduced-allergen menu for listed ingredients; confirm with hotel if applicable. Hotel overview.
