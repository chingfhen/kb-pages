One-line route and how to get there

Hotel / Asakusabashi → JR Kamakura Station → Enoden to Hase → Hasedera + Great Buddha → Enoden westbound toward Enoshima → Enoshima Island → return from Fujisawa / Ofuna to Tokyo and Asakusabashi.

Outbound train: From ICI HOTEL Asakusabashi walk to JR Asakusabashi (~2 min West Exit). Take JR Chuo-Sobu Local one stop to Akihabara, then JR towards Tokyo Station and connect to JR Yokosuka Line bound for Kamakura/Zushi. A journey via Shinagawa can be equally convenient: use Google Maps to choose the lowest-transfer connection available that morning. Target Kamakura Station, allow roughly 75–100 minutes from hotel with transfers. Suica is fine; no rail pass booked / required.

At Kamakura, transfer from JR to the adjacent Enoshima Electric Railway (Enoden) platform; take Enoden towards Fujisawa and get off at Hase Station (~5 min). Enoden accepts Suica, and the coastal ride later is an experience in itself. Enoden official route/access.

Recommended anchors: why, order and flexibility

Anchor (Google Maps) Why you're going What to do / effort
1. Hasedera / 長谷寺 Beautiful hillside Buddhist temple, statues and landscaped grounds, with coastal views; best temple/nature blend Core, 45–75 min; 5-minute walk from Hase Station; some stairs.
2. Kotoku-in Great Buddha / 高徳院 Iconic 11m+ bronze Amida Buddha standing in the open air; a historic symbol of Kamakura Core, 25–45 min; roughly 10 min walk from Hase Station; short stroll from Hasedera.
3. Enoden coastal train / 江ノ電 Nostalgic small train running past homes and Sagami Bay shoreline; not just transport Core ride from Hase toward Enoshima (~20 min). Kamakura-koko-mae Station is an optional brief photo stop, not essential; stay off roads/rail crossings.
4. Enoshima Island / 江の島 Seaside shrine island with street food, ocean viewpoints and possible Fuji silhouette in clear skies Core if weather/energy cooperate, 90–150 min. From Enoden Enoshima Station walk approximately 15–25 min including footbridge to the island entrance. Lots of steps uphill.
5. Enoshima Shrine / 江島神社 Historic sea/island shrine complex with multiple sacred halls across the slope Selected within island, take your time; optional paid Escar escalators reduce ascent effort.
6. Sea Candle / 江の島シーキャンドル Elevated seaside/lighthouse panorama; best if views are clear and energy good Optional paid add-on, not a required ticket; tower hours normally 09:00–20:00, check day/event changes.

Don't bolt on distant Kamakura attractions simply because they're famous. Komachi-dori and Tsurugaoka Hachimangu near Kamakura Station make a separate optional morning/afternoon town cluster if you replace Enoshima, not extra stops in the same full day.

Leaving Enoshima: From Enoden Enoshima Station you can continue Enoden to Fujisawa, then JR Tokaido Line toward Tokyo/Shinagawa and onward to Akihabara / Asakusabashi by the most convenient JR connection. Another option is Shonan-Enoshima monorail to Ofuna, then JR Yokosuka/Tokaido service back to Tokyo. Use Google Maps from your actual last location; no need to retrace all the way to Kamakura. Food and low-effort swaps

  • Kamakura shirasu-don / しらす丼 — rice bowl topped with whitebait, local coastal speciality; availability and cooked/raw varieties vary with season. Find one near your actual lunch point.
  • Enoshima seafood / 江の島 海鮮 — island-side seafood meals or street snacks.
  • Yoridokoro / ヨリドコロ — well-known cosy grilled-fish cafe overlooking the passing Enoden train (not a sea-view cafe); consider only if you pass, it's open and queues are reasonable, not a detour to preserve.

Weather flexibility: If wind/rain makes Enoshima unpleasant, skip the island and enjoy a slower Kamakura/Hase temple-and-café day. If excellent visibility, prioritize the island and avoid Komachi-dori detours. Enoshima's sea-facing paths and outdoor viewpoint are affected by conditions; daylight fades early in November. Enoshima Sea Candle official operating hours.

Costs / passes: JR and Enoden ordinary rail fares are payable with Suica. Enoden has an optional ¥800 Noriorikun day pass in prior research, but 2–3 short Enoden rides may cost less; do not buy a pass pre-emptively. Pay individual temple/observation admissions if you actually go.
