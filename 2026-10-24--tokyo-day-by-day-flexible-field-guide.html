Thursday 12 Nov — arrive Narita, easy Roppongi afternoon/evening

What matters: The two confirmed arriving flights reach Narita Terminal 1 about 07:10 and 07:30 respectively. Meet after your own entry formalities in a pre-agreed public T1 Arrivals meeting area (arrival exit/terminal level; don't assume you can wait airside together). Immigration, bags, finding each other and hotel check-in are buffers, not appointments. remm Roppongi is booked for nights 12–14 Nov.

Suggested airport-to-hotel rail route (simple, not the only route):

  1. Follow Keisei Line / Skyliner signs in T1 to Narita Airport Station. Purchase a Skyliner reserved seat ticket to Keisei-Ueno (Skyliner surcharge is not covered by Suica alone).
  2. At Keisei-Ueno, follow pedestrian signs to Tokyo Metro Ueno Station (the buildings/stations are distinct). Take the Hibiya Line towards Naka-meguro and get off at Roppongi. This is a through Hibiya ride from Ueno, with no other train transfer.
  3. Walk approximately one minute from Roppongi Hibiya exit 4b to remm Roppongi. 4b is currently cited by the hotel on its English access page; signs or construction may change. Allow roughly 90–120 minutes after boarding the first train including transfer/wait; not a commitment tied to arrival time.
  4. Ask the front desk to hold luggage if the room is not ready; ordinary check-in time must be confirmed with hotel. Rest and eat nearby instead of forcing a full sightseeing day.

If easier with luggage: Narita Express → Tokyo Station → Marunouchi Line to Kasumigaseki → Hibiya Line to Roppongi is described in hotel-area access guidance, but entails more station navigation. Check route options in Google Maps on arrival, including any current disruption. Official airport train information · Keisei Skyliner.

Saturday 14 Nov — daytime flexible, Koishikawa garden evening selected

One fixed-ish anchor: Koishikawa Korakuen West Gate around 18:00–19:30 for the paid flexible-date night-garden ticket. Travel by metro/JR towards Iidabashi; the West Gate, not East Gate, is the event entrance. Arrive around 17:45–18:00. Present the night-garden voucher; only swipe to redeem with the staff. Check that the voucher quantity matches the party before arrival. Confirm the 2026 event details before travel.

Choose your daytime area only when awake: stay near Roppongi; alternatively walk Shinjuku Gyoen or do one Tokyo neighbourhood from the area menu.

Natural optional dinner after the garden: Kagurazaka Hyogo Yokocho for lantern-lit historical side alleys and restaurants; it's convenient from the Iidabashi side. If you already explore Kagurazaka tonight, there's no need to repeat it on 20 Nov. No extra reservation.

Tuesday 17 Nov — back from Hakone; simple hotel arrival

The day already has a hard commitment: Hakone Yuryo Kikyo private bath 13:00–15:00, then collect suitcases by 18:00 at Hakone-Yumoto station storage. Full logistics and shuttle are in the separate Hakone guide.

After bath: Take an ordinary Odakyu service covered by the returning Shinjuku-origin Hakone Freepass from Hakone-Yumoto via Odawara to Shinjuku (check actual train); no Romancecar supplement is bought for this direction. From Shinjuku take JR Chuo-Sobu local eastbound toward Asakusabashi, typically via a direct local or a connection depending on the train. Enter destination in Maps as ICI HOTEL Asakusabashi. The hotel is about 2 min from JR Asakusabashi West Exit or 4 min from Asakusa subway A3. Unpack, rest; nearby Asakusabashi izakaya search is optional. Don't add obligatory activities after the bath.

  • Garden Place Tower 38F SKY LOUNGE — optional free indoor city view.

If sky entry late or wife's shopping runs long: move Ebisu to a free evening on 18/20 Nov. The two experiences remain selected but their clock times are not rigid.

Default walk: Yanaka + Nezu (old Tokyo without an exhausting excursion)

From Asakusabashi, take JR Chuo-Sobu to Akihabara, then JR Yamanote or Keihin-Tohoku northbound to Nippori (check Google Maps for most convenient train). Route: Nippori West Exit → Yuyake Dandan sunset stairs → Yanaka Ginza shopping street → optional Kayaba Coffee / Ueno Sakuragi Atari → Nezu Shrine. Nezu Shrine's red torii, ponds and historic buildings are worth the visit; the azalea blooms are spring, not November. Navigate home from Nezu or Nippori; no need to walk back to the start. Snack/stroll/coffee as you wish. Official Tokyo Yanaka + Nezu guide.

Alternative walk: Kagurazaka (very flexible, especially after lunch)

From Asakusabashi take JR Chuo-Sobu Local westbound to Iidabashi, then Kagurazaka main slope → Bishamonten Zenkoku-ji Temple → Hyogo Yokocho / Kakurenbo Yokocho → optional Akagi-jinja Shrine. Why: traditional lanes and former geisha-district architecture with stylish cafés and small bistros. 2–4h, easy but hilly streets. GO TOKYO official walk.

If you decide to do Mt Takao after all — optional route and effort

No booking necessary for ordinary trains or cable car. From Asakusabashi, use JR Chuo-Sobu to Shinjuku, then Keio Line / Takao Line to Takaosanguchi (approximately 50–60 min from Shinjuku; some services involve a transfer at Kitano). Allow ~90–120 min each way from hotel including transfers on a normal day; autumn congestion adds time. Keio Takaosanguchi Google Maps.

Gentle summit version: Takaosanguchi → ~5 min walk Kiyotaki cable-car station → cable car to Takaosan Station (about 6 min) → follow Trail 1, stop at Yakuoin Temple (Tengu iconography, mountain worship) → Mt Takao summit (599m, possible Fuji view) → retrace Trail 1 to cable car down. Important: cable car does not reach summit; there are still slopes/stairs and approximately 40–60 min walking each way from upper station including temple and pauses. Or do cable car + scenic viewpoints/temple only, skip summit for a lighter half-day on the mountain (still a long commute). No need for hard Trail 6 or Inariyama.

Official cable car details observed Oct 2026: adult ¥490 one-way / ¥980 return; weekday November last cable car typically 17:45 (can change), chairlift ends around 16:30, so don't rely on a late descent. Official operator timetable · 2026 autumn event. Choose Takao only if the forecast looks good and you're happy spending most of the final day there; crowds may add significant queues. No reservation now.

Recommended Shinjuku pairing: Omoide Yokocho for an early dinner → stroll to Golden Gai for one drink. Convenient as a 13 Nov optional detour on the return from Kawagoe (if routed via Shinjuku), or on another uncommitted evening. Neither stop is locked.

Ebisu pairing: Keep 19 Nov Shibuya shopping → selected Ebisu Garden Place lights first; then consider Ebisu Yokocho for dinner if hungry and energetic. Ebisu Yokocho is nearer Ebisu Station, not inside Garden Place. Do not force both Shinjuku and Ebisu nightlife into the same evening.

Practical cautions: Typical total spend varies by establishment and drinks ordered; use posted menus rather than assuming a fixed price. Golden Gai's tiny bars may charge a cover/otōshi, have limited seating or rules on photos. Izakaya venues can be crowded; check hours and queues on the day.

Important etiquette: Golden Gai micro-bars may require cover charge / otōshi in addition to drinks; check before sitting, bring some cash, don't follow street touts and respect no-photo signs. Official Golden Gai explanation. No restaurant bookings required; avoid trying to cover all five nights.

Saturday 21 Nov — check out and fly from Narita T1

This is a travel day, not a sightseeing day. The 17:00 ZIPAIR return to Bangkok (BKK) is earlier than the 19:55 Scoot service to Singapore (via Taipei). Both are scheduled from Narita Terminal 1; recheck boarding passes for exact counters and flight numbers. Aim to arrive NRT T1 around 13:45–14:00 for the earlier flight. ZIPAIR officially opens counter check-in 3 hours before and closes it 1 hour before; do not rely on arriving shortly before closing. There will be extra airport time before the later Scoot departure.

Best simple station-to-airport choice with luggage: From ICI HOTEL Asakusabashi (about 4 min walk from Toei Asakusa Asakusabashi A3), check Google Maps for a direct through Keisei / Toei Asakusa Airport Access Express to Narita Airport T1 (成田空港). Some departure times offer a no-transfer service, others require changing trains; inspect the particular Saturday timetable and train destination on the day. Allow ~70–100 minutes station to airport if direct, plus waiting and walk. This is a normal commuter-style train with luggage, not the reserved Skyliner; ensure the destination really says Narita Airport, not Haneda. Official Toei airport connectivity · Keisei Access Express.

Backup (comfort/clearer train times): JR from Asakusabashi → Akihabara, then JR → Ueno / Nippori, walk to Keisei-Ueno or transfer at Nippori, and take a reserved-seat Skyliner to Narita Airport T1. More transfers and a higher fare, but more reliable seat/luggage space. Keisei timetable.

Practical time target: Check out and start heading to Asakusabashi Station around 11:30–12:00, earlier if your chosen train requires changes or baggage makes transfer slow. Use Narita Airport Terminal 1 departures as the navigation target. ZIPAIR uses Terminal 1 North Wing, 4F. Both parties keep passports, boarding credentials, and airline-specific baggage allowances handy. Narita ZIPAIR terminal · ZIPAIR check-in rules.

One actionable baggage check before departure: The ZIPAIR allowance is 7 kg total carry-on, no checked baggage. ZIPAIR allows two cabin pieces totalling 7 kg; paid upgrade to 15 kg cabin total needs advance purchase at least 24 hours before flight, and checked luggage is a paid separate add-on. Confirm whether the actual suitcase needs checking, and buy any needed allowance in advance rather than finding out at the airport. Official ZIPAIR baggage policy. Verify the Scoot allowance separately.
