Transport: Suica, no additional sightseeing rail pass

Leave remm Roppongi around 08:15 and check the real 13 Nov morning train on Google Maps. Suica works for ordinary Tokyo rail and compatible buses; flexibility wins over a Tobu-only pass for a day spent mostly on foot.

  • Via Seibu/Shinjuku: Toei Oedo Line from Roppongi to Shinjuku, walk separately to Seibu-Shinjuku Station, Seibu Line to Hon-Kawagoe; the closest rail arrival for Wargo, but allow walking time at Shinjuku.
  • Via Tobu/Ikebukuro: transfer to Ikebukuro then Tobu Tojo Line to Kawagoe Station; longer walk to the rental shop. Choose whichever Google Maps transit connection is better that morning.
  • Local bus: useful mainly after Hikawa Shrine if tired in kimono sandals; find a stop/service toward 本川越駅 / Hon-Kawagoe and check the weekday schedule. For IC-accepted buses, tap at the displayed boarding/exit readers (some buses use two taps). Five classic saved stops, with significance

The old town is meant to be walked—browsing façades, food shops and taking kimono photographs. Do not rush through as five unrelated photo checkboxes. Walk the compact Taisho Roman → Kurazukuri → Bell Tower → Candy Alley cluster; Hikawa Shrine is farther. Expect several kilometres across a full day with detours; a bus return can reduce fatigue.

Anchor Why it is worth seeing Use it
1. Taisho Roman Yume-dori / 大正浪漫夢通り Nostalgic early-20th-century architecture, small cafés and stores Optional first strolling/photo stop, ~15–30 min
2. Kurazukuri / Ichibangai / 蔵造りの町並み Kawagoe's signature dark merchant warehouses and traditional shopfronts; main kimono photo setting Core destination; wander freely
3. Toki no Kane / 時の鐘 Signature wooden bell tower and recognizable Kawagoe photo backdrop Core quick stop, bell typically rings at noon and 15:00
4. Kashiya Yokocho / 菓子屋横丁 Compact traditional sweets alley with nostalgic confectionery Core snacks stop, ~20–40 min
5. Hikawa Shrine / 川越氷川神社 Shrine associated with matchmaking and lasting relationships, especially meaningful for a couple Core shrine, bus back toward Wargo if needed

Alternatives, not additional obligations: Kawagoe Kumano Shrine (fortunes and luck), Kita-in Temple (Buddhist/Edo history and stone statues), Kawagoe Castle Honmaru Palace (samurai-era architecture). Shingashi River is better known for spring blossoms; Hikawa wind-chimes are a summer event, not November. JNTO old town itinerary.
